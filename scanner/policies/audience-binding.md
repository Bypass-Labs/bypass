# Automated communication audience binding

Invariant: email, messaging, webhook, export, and other automated delivery of
protected content must remain bound to a verified, currently authorized audience
and purpose from configuration through the moment of send.

Trace recipient values, subscriptions, workflow ownership, templates, protected
content, preview endpoints, scheduled execution, and delivery credentials.
Inspect verification on recipient changes, tenant or account transitions,
forwarding destinations, unsubscribe and revocation, retry behavior, blind-copy
or group expansion, and whether logs reveal the content or address. Syntax
validation alone does not establish audience authority. Account-bound verified
destinations and reauthorization before delivery are counterevidence.

Candidates must identify who can set the audience, what protected content is
delivered, and the send path with citations. Separate intentional user-directed
sharing from stale or attacker-controlled delivery. Do not send a message or
subscribe a destination.
