# Personal-data derivative lifecycle

Invariant: consent, purpose, retention, export, correction, and deletion rules
for personal or confidential source data must propagate to all derivatives,
including model prompts and outputs, embeddings, summaries, caches, analytics,
exports, backups, logs, and third-party processor copies.

Build a source-to-derivative lineage from collection and inferred attributes to
every persistence and disclosure. Inspect whether derivatives retain subject and
purpose identity, whether correction or deletion invalidates cached and queued
work, provider retention settings, backup expiry, legal holds, shared aggregates,
and re-derivation after deletion. Deleting only the source row is insufficient
when useful personal content survives elsewhere. Irreversible aggregation and
documented processor deletion are relevant counterevidence.

Candidates must cite the protected source, derivative, lifecycle operation, and
surviving path. Separate regulatory conclusions from source-observed technical
gaps and state the applicable product requirement as an assumption. Do not read
excluded personal data or call a provider.
